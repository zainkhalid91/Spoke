03 · Sub-actions fan out (slice “Search ▸”)
Hover a ▸ slice for 150 ms (or press →) and up to 4 petals fan out beyond its rim. The main ring stays visible, so you never lose your place.
Petals are numbered 1–4 while the fan is open. ← or moving back onto the ring collapses it.
Alternative per slice: Run all. Clicking runs all children in order (hub shows 1/3, 2/3, 3/3).
After choosing YOUTUBE, a small query box appears where the hub was. It's the only moment Spoke takes focus; Enter opens results and focus returns.
