01 · Wheel open — default (nothing hovered)
Opens centred on the pointer on the monitor under it, at that monitor's DPI. Clamped so it never goes off-screen.

Notepad keeps focus: its caret is still blinking behind the wheel.
Numbers 1–8 on the inner edge = keyboard shortcuts.
“▸” on a slice = it has sub-actions.
Wheel row: other wheels. Tab / Shift+Tab or click to switch in place.
Light dim behind (optional, Appearance). Click anywhere outside the ring with nothing hovered = close.
